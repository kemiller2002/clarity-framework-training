Engagement is intentionally simple. No funnel. No pressure. Just fit and readiness.

How it works

  • Request: short intake about the decision and stakes
  • Fit call: 15–30 minutes to confirm readiness
  • Schedule: cohort seat or private team session

We will say “no”

  • If you want validation for a decision already made
  • If you want templates instead of judgment
  • If the decision is primarily political or value-conflicted

A direct “no” is part of maintaining integrity.

Request an Invite

The online intake is not open yet. This site does not collect or send requests, so there is no form to submit here.

When the intake opens, it will ask for:

  • Your name and email
  • Your role or title, and your organization
  • The decision you are facing, and why it is hard
  • What is at stake: the cost of being wrong, reversibility and timing
  • The current failure mode: debate, misalignment, uncertainty, urgency, missing data or politics
